# GMA Sales Acquisition Playbook

Purpose: practical outbound and inbound acquisition methods for Granite Models Automations focused on proving value before pitching, generating qualified conversations, and creating repeatable sales channels for automation, backend integration, CRM, workflow, AI-agent, and software services.

## 1. Broken-Form Loom Sniper Method

### Action
Open Google Maps and search for high-ticket local service businesses in major metro areas, especially:

- Roofers
- Commercial HVAC companies
- Med spas
- Custom builders
- Other service businesses where one missed lead can represent substantial revenue

Click through to each business website and inspect its lead-capture/contact flow.

### Execution
Test the lead capture or contact form using truthful, non-disruptive information. Observe whether:

- The form is slow
- The submission fails
- The user lands on a generic thank-you page with no useful confirmation
- No immediate acknowledgement is sent
- No fast routing or alert appears to exist
- The follow-up experience is visibly weak

When a real funnel leak is found, record a roughly 90-second Loom video that:

1. Shows the exact point of friction or failure on screen.
2. Explains why it can cost the business qualified leads.
3. Shows a short live example of how the form could be connected to a Telegram or Slack alert.
4. Shows how an instant automated SMS acknowledgement could be triggered.
5. Ends with a specific offer to fix that exact problem.

### Why It Works
This is not an abstract automation pitch. The prospect sees a live weakness in its own lead funnel and a concrete implementation path to close it.

### Operating Rule
Do not fabricate problems, flood forms, impersonate customers, or create operational disruption. The value proposition depends on demonstrating a real, observable leak.

## 2. Agency White-Label Backdoor

### Action
Find:

- Freelance web designers
- Shopify developers
- Boutique marketing agencies
- Small web-development studios
- Front-end-focused implementation shops

Use LinkedIn, X, local directories, agency directories, and relevant professional communities.

### Execution
Target agencies that are strong at client-facing design and implementation but may not want to spend internal development time on:

- Webhook failures
- API integrations
- Rate-limit handling
- CRM data mapping
- Backend workflow automation
- Make/Zapier implementation
- Cross-system data synchronization
- Custom glue code

Use a direct positioning message such as:

> Hey [Name], I noticed you build great Shopify stores. If you ever run into a client who needs messy backend automation, CRM data syncs, or custom Make/Zapier glue work that eats up your development hours, I do white-label backend execution. You bill the client whatever you want; I’ll handle the logic behind the scenes.

### Why It Works
The agency keeps the client relationship and margin while expanding what it can sell. GMA becomes the backend execution layer instead of competing for the end-client directly.

A productive agency relationship can generate multiple projects from one acquisition effort.

### Sales-System Implication
Treat agencies as channel partners, not one-off leads. Track partner source, capability demand, project volume, close rate, average project value, repeat work, and delivery quality separately from direct customers.

## 3. Public Forum Rescue Mission

### Action
Monitor communities where users are actively asking for workflow help, including:

- Make Community
- Zapier Community
- r/zapier
- r/nocode
- Other automation, integration, CRM, and workflow communities

Prioritize new or unanswered posts describing active problems.

### Execution
Look for issues such as:

- Broken JSON parsing
- Date-format errors
- Webhook payload mapping failures
- API authentication problems
- CRM field mismatches
- Automation loops
- Failed routing logic
- Broken conditional branches

Provide the exact functional solution publicly when possible. The goal is to solve the immediate technical problem rather than post a generic service advertisement.

Where appropriate, explain:

1. What is actually failing.
2. Why it is failing.
3. The exact logic or configuration needed to repair it.
4. What should be tested afterward.

### Why It Works
The audience already has the problem, already understands the cost of the failure, and can evaluate technical competence from the answer itself. Some users will decide they do not want to maintain the workflow and will seek implementation help.

### Operating Rule
Lead with the solution. Do not turn technical communities into repetitive promotional channels. Reputation is the acquisition mechanism.

## 4. Local B2B Facebook & Skool Community Infiltration

### Action
Join communities where owners and operators discuss real operating problems, including:

- Local business-owner groups
- Contractor networks
- Trade-specific groups
- Industry Facebook groups
- Relevant Skool communities

Monitor for terms such as:

- CRM
- Leads
- Manual
- Spreadsheet
- QuickBooks
- Follow-up
- Scheduling
- Estimates
- Invoices
- Data entry
- Automation

### Execution
Do not post generic messages such as "I do automation."

When a member describes a repetitive or expensive workflow problem, reply with a precise breakdown of the logic required to solve it. For example, if a business is spending hours moving invoice data into spreadsheets, explain the workflow that could automatically capture, validate, transform, and route the information.

A suitable close is:

> If you want me to spin this up for you so you never touch it again, shoot me a DM.

### Why It Works
Useful technical answers create trust inside communities where direct promotion is often ignored or removed. The public answer establishes competence; the commercial discussion moves to direct messages after value has already been demonstrated.

### Operating Rule
Respect each community's rules. Do not evade moderation or disguise spam as help. The method depends on being genuinely useful enough that the answer stands on its own.

## 5. Agent-Driven Programmatic SEO & Content Scale

### Action
Use the GMA agent workforce to build a large, structured library of highly specific technical landing pages and support content targeting long-tail searches that traditional agencies rarely pursue economically.

Examples include:

- "Sync Airtable to custom legacy SQL via webhook"
- "HubSpot to QuickBooks custom field mapping"
- "Fix Make JSON parser invalid array error"
- "Route Typeform leads to Telegram and CRM"
- "Connect legacy contractor software to modern APIs"

### Execution — Long-Tail Landing Pages

Agents should identify integration pairs, error classes, workflow problems, industries, and software combinations with credible search intent, then create useful landing pages around them.

Each page should contain:

1. The exact problem or integration being targeted.
2. Who commonly needs it.
3. The systems involved.
4. A technically credible implementation path.
5. Constraints, failure modes, or edge cases.
6. A clear service CTA tied to that specific problem.
7. Internal links to related integrations, tutorials, demos, and service pages.

Do not publish hundreds of thin pages containing only swapped keywords. Pages must be materially useful and distinct enough to deserve indexing.

### Execution — Automated Technical Documentation & Blogs

Use agents to create deep technical tutorials around real implementation problems such as:

- API errors
- Authentication failures
- JSON parsing issues
- Webhook debugging
- CRM field mapping
- Data transformation
- Rate limits
- Retry logic
- Integration edge cases

These articles should solve the searcher's immediate problem while naturally exposing the related GMA implementation service.

### Why It Works
Long-tail technical searches often represent users who already have a defined problem. GMA can produce technically specific content at a breadth and speed that would be expensive for a conventional agency.

### Operating Rule
Programmatic does not mean unreviewed. Require source grounding, duplicate-content checks, factual verification, technical QA, canonicalization, index controls, and measurable search intent before publication.

## 6. Autonomous Business Build-in-Public Persona

### Action
Turn the operation of GMA itself into a marketing asset. Publish selected evidence showing how the agent workforce researches, builds, verifies, routes, and completes work under human governance.

### Execution — Live Agent Logs on X and LinkedIn

Publish approved or semi-automated content showing examples such as:

- Agents diagnosing workflow failures
- Agents building integrations
- Agents performing QA or certification
- Agent-to-agent handoffs
- Conduit routing work between specialists
- Sales research and opportunity qualification
- Before/after process metrics

Prefer short evidence-driven threads, clips, screenshots, diagrams, and measured results over generic AI commentary.

Do not expose customer secrets, credentials, proprietary client logic, private communications, or internal security details.

### Execution — Human-in-the-Loop Positioning

Position GMA around orchestration and governed automation rather than pretending no human oversight exists.

Useful proof points include metrics such as:

- Jobs processed by agents
- Tasks automatically routed
- Percentage completed without manual intervention
- Human approval time
- QA pass rates
- Rework rates
- Turnaround improvements
- Cost-per-task reductions

Example positioning:

> Agents processed 400 qualified operations today. Human approval and exception handling took 7 minutes.

Only publish numbers backed by system telemetry.

### Why It Works
The operating model is itself differentiated. Showing the system working creates curiosity, demonstrates technical capability, and gives founders concrete evidence of what an agent-driven company can actually accomplish.

### Operating Rule
Build in public selectively. Transparency should increase trust without exposing security boundaries, customer data, internal credentials, confidential contracts, or unverified performance claims.

## 7. AI & Agent Directory Submissions

### Action
List GMA, its autonomous service platform, and qualifying products on discovery channels used by AI-native founders, developers, and early adopters.

Potential channels include:

- AI product directories
- Agent directories
- Automation directories
- Founder communities
- Software marketplaces
- Futurepedia or similar discovery platforms where appropriate
- Product Hunt for launch-worthy products or client-facing experiences

### Execution
Create a reusable listing packet containing:

- One-line positioning
- Short description
- Long description
- Product screenshots
- Demo video
- Primary use cases
- Core differentiators
- Pricing or CTA
- Founder/company profile
- Tracking URL or campaign code

Maintain a directory registry so the sales system knows where GMA is listed, when the listing was updated, traffic generated, leads produced, and revenue attributed.

### Product Hunt
When a client portal, automation product, or agent workflow has a credible standalone user experience, package the launch around one understandable outcome rather than presenting the entire GMA architecture at once.

### Why It Works
Directories concentrate users who are actively searching for new software and AI-native solutions. A standardized listing pipeline turns them into a low-cost discovery channel rather than an occasional manual launch task.

## 8. Automated Inbound Interception

### Action
Create a dedicated sales-intelligence sub-agent that monitors permitted public sources for people actively describing automation failures, integration problems, repetitive manual work, or broken software workflows.

Potential sources include:

- Reddit
- X
- Developer communities
- Public support forums
- Automation communities
- Public issue/discussion boards where outreach is appropriate

### Execution
The agent should:

1. Collect posts through approved APIs, search tools, feeds, or other platform-permitted access methods.
2. Classify whether the post represents a real commercial or technical problem.
3. Extract the software involved, failure mode, urgency, and likely business impact.
4. Score the opportunity against GMA capabilities and sales criteria.
5. Draft a useful, technically specific response.
6. Place the draft, source link, evidence, and opportunity score into the sales dashboard.
7. Require one-click human approval before publishing or initiating contact unless a future policy explicitly authorizes that channel for autonomous response.
8. Record the published response and resulting conversation back into the opportunity ledger.

### Why It Works
This converts scattered public complaints into a continuously refreshed demand signal. Instead of waiting for prospects to discover GMA, the sales system identifies users at the moment they are actively trying to solve a problem.

### Operating Rule
Do not bypass platform controls, evade rate limits, mass-spam users, or collect restricted/private data. The agent should optimize for high-relevance intervention rather than maximum message volume.

# GMA Sales Integration Requirements

These methods should feed the broader GMA sales system rather than operate as disconnected manual tactics.

For every opportunity, capture at minimum:

- Acquisition method
- Lead or partner source
- Company / contact
- Industry
- Observed problem
- Evidence captured
- Estimated business impact
- Proposed solution
- Product/service category
- Estimated project value
- Contact status
- Demo or Loom URL if applicable
- Source content URL if applicable
- Search query / keyword if applicable
- Agent confidence or qualification score
- Human approval state
- Next action
- Outcome
- Lost reason if rejected
- Repeat/referral potential

## Recommended Opportunity Types

Use distinct classifications so performance can be measured independently:

- `DIRECT_FUNNEL_REPAIR`
- `AGENCY_WHITE_LABEL_PARTNER`
- `PUBLIC_FORUM_INBOUND`
- `COMMUNITY_ASSIST_TO_DM`
- `PROGRAMMATIC_SEO_INBOUND`
- `BUILD_IN_PUBLIC_INBOUND`
- `DIRECTORY_DISCOVERY`
- `AUTONOMOUS_INTERCEPTION`

## Core Metrics

Track:

- Prospects researched
- Qualified problems found
- Looms/videos sent
- Replies
- Meetings or DMs started
- Quotes/SOWs issued
- Wins
- Losses
- Revenue by acquisition method
- Time spent per won deal
- Repeat work
- Partner-sourced revenue
- Organic search impressions
- Organic clicks
- Indexed landing pages
- Leads per landing page
- Technical-content assisted conversions
- Build-in-public impressions and qualified replies
- Directory referral traffic and conversions
- Intercepted opportunities
- Human-approved outbound responses
- Response-to-conversation rate
- Conversation-to-sale rate

The goal is to identify which acquisition channel produces the highest qualified revenue per hour and progressively automate research, routing, evidence capture, content production, opportunity scoring, follow-up, and reporting without weakening human approval over customer commitments or publication quality.
