# GMA Sales Acquisition Playbook

Purpose: practical outbound acquisition methods for Granite Models Automations focused on proving value before pitching, generating qualified conversations, and creating repeatable sales channels for automation, backend integration, CRM, workflow, and software services.

## 1. Broken-Form Loom Sniper Method

### Action
Open Google Maps and search for high-ticket local service businesses in major metro areas, especially:

- Roofers
- Commercial HVAC companies
- Med spas
- Custom builders
- Other service businesses where one missed lead can represent substantial revenue

Click through to each business website and inspect its lead-capture/contact flow.

### Execution
Test the lead capture or contact form using truthful, non-disruptive information. Observe whether:

- The form is slow
- The submission fails
- The user lands on a generic thank-you page with no useful confirmation
- No immediate acknowledgement is sent
- No fast routing or alert appears to exist
- The follow-up experience is visibly weak

When a real funnel leak is found, record a roughly 90-second Loom video that:

1. Shows the exact point of friction or failure on screen.
2. Explains why it can cost the business qualified leads.
3. Shows a short live example of how the form could be connected to a Telegram or Slack alert.
4. Shows how an instant automated SMS acknowledgement could be triggered.
5. Ends with a specific offer to fix that exact problem.

### Why It Works
This is not an abstract automation pitch. The prospect sees a live weakness in its own lead funnel and a concrete implementation path to close it.

### Operating Rule
Do not fabricate problems, flood forms, impersonate customers, or create operational disruption. The value proposition depends on demonstrating a real, observable leak.

## 2. Agency White-Label Backdoor

### Action
Find:

- Freelance web designers
- Shopify developers
- Boutique marketing agencies
- Small web-development studios
- Front-end-focused implementation shops

Use LinkedIn, X, local directories, agency directories, and relevant professional communities.

### Execution
Target agencies that are strong at client-facing design and implementation but may not want to spend internal development time on:

- Webhook failures
- API integrations
- Rate-limit handling
- CRM data mapping
- Backend workflow automation
- Make/Zapier implementation
- Cross-system data synchronization
- Custom glue code

Use a direct positioning message such as:

> Hey [Name], I noticed you build great Shopify stores. If you ever run into a client who needs messy backend automation, CRM data syncs, or custom Make/Zapier glue work that eats up your development hours, I do white-label backend execution. You bill the client whatever you want; I’ll handle the logic behind the scenes.

### Why It Works
The agency keeps the client relationship and margin while expanding what it can sell. GMA becomes the backend execution layer instead of competing for the end-client directly.

A productive agency relationship can generate multiple projects from one acquisition effort.

### Sales-System Implication
Treat agencies as channel partners, not one-off leads. Track partner source, capability demand, project volume, close rate, average project value, repeat work, and delivery quality separately from direct customers.

## 3. Public Forum Rescue Mission

### Action
Monitor communities where users are actively asking for workflow help, including:

- Make Community
- Zapier Community
- r/zapier
- r/nocode
- Other automation, integration, CRM, and workflow communities

Prioritize new or unanswered posts describing active problems.

### Execution
Look for issues such as:

- Broken JSON parsing
- Date-format errors
- Webhook payload mapping failures
- API authentication problems
- CRM field mismatches
- Automation loops
- Failed routing logic
- Broken conditional branches

Provide the exact functional solution publicly when possible. The goal is to solve the immediate technical problem rather than post a generic service advertisement.

Where appropriate, explain:

1. What is actually failing.
2. Why it is failing.
3. The exact logic or configuration needed to repair it.
4. What should be tested afterward.

### Why It Works
The audience already has the problem, already understands the cost of the failure, and can evaluate technical competence from the answer itself. Some users will decide they do not want to maintain the workflow and will seek implementation help.

### Operating Rule
Lead with the solution. Do not turn technical communities into repetitive promotional channels. Reputation is the acquisition mechanism.

## 4. Local B2B Facebook & Skool Community Infiltration

### Action
Join communities where owners and operators discuss real operating problems, including:

- Local business-owner groups
- Contractor networks
- Trade-specific groups
- Industry Facebook groups
- Relevant Skool communities

Monitor for terms such as:

- CRM
- Leads
- Manual
- Spreadsheet
- QuickBooks
- Follow-up
- Scheduling
- Estimates
- Invoices
- Data entry
- Automation

### Execution
Do not post generic messages such as "I do automation."

When a member describes a repetitive or expensive workflow problem, reply with a precise breakdown of the logic required to solve it. For example, if a business is spending hours moving invoice data into spreadsheets, explain the workflow that could automatically capture, validate, transform, and route the information.

A suitable close is:

> If you want me to spin this up for you so you never touch it again, shoot me a DM.

### Why It Works
Useful technical answers create trust inside communities where direct promotion is often ignored or removed. The public answer establishes competence; the commercial discussion moves to direct messages after value has already been demonstrated.

### Operating Rule
Respect each community's rules. Do not evade moderation or disguise spam as help. The method depends on being genuinely useful enough that the answer stands on its own.

# GMA Sales Integration Requirements

These methods should feed the broader GMA sales system rather than operate as disconnected manual tactics.

For every opportunity, capture at minimum:

- Acquisition method
- Lead or partner source
- Company / contact
- Industry
- Observed problem
- Evidence captured
- Estimated business impact
- Proposed solution
- Product/service category
- Estimated project value
- Contact status
- Demo or Loom URL if applicable
- Next action
- Outcome
- Lost reason if rejected
- Repeat/referral potential

## Recommended Opportunity Types

Use distinct classifications so performance can be measured independently:

- `DIRECT_FUNNEL_REPAIR`
- `AGENCY_WHITE_LABEL_PARTNER`
- `PUBLIC_FORUM_INBOUND`
- `COMMUNITY_ASSIST_TO_DM`

## Core Metrics

Track:

- Prospects researched
- Qualified problems found
- Looms/videos sent
- Replies
- Meetings or DMs started
- Quotes/SOWs issued
- Wins
- Losses
- Revenue by acquisition method
- Time spent per won deal
- Repeat work
- Partner-sourced revenue

The goal is to identify which acquisition channel produces the highest qualified revenue per hour and progressively automate research, routing, evidence capture, follow-up, and reporting without weakening human approval over customer commitments.
